How You Play

Four ways to get Trivd.

⚡
Speed Duel
1v1 · First correct answer wins

Race head-to-head. First player to tap the right answer takes the round. Fastest fingers win — knowledge helps, but reflexes matter.

🎯
Accuracy Mode
Multiplayer · Most correct answers wins

Pick your rounds (3, 5, or 7), share a room code, and go. Points for every correct answer. Streaks multiply your score. Leaderboard updates live.

🎲
Dare Mode
2–6 players · Punishments for last place every round.

Pass the phone around the table. No app download required for other players. Dare the player in last place every round. Perfect for parties, trivia nights, or anywhere the stakes should be higher.

💥
Chaos Mode
Power-ups · Steal points · Mayhem

Power-ups, point steals, and total mayhem. Everything you thought you knew about trivia goes out the window. Friendships tested. Legends made.
