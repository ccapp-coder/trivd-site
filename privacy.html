Privacy Policy

Courtright Collective LLC · Last updated 2026-10-26

Effective 2025-01-01

Trivd is a trivia app developed and published by Courtright Collective LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Trivd app or the Trivd website tools at trivd.courtrightco.com.

The short version: we don't sell your data, we don't track you across apps, and you can play without ever giving us your name or email.

Information You Provide

Trivd is designed to work without requiring an account. When you play without signing in:

  • Your stats and streak data are stored locally on your device only
  • No personal information is sent to our servers
  • Game data is not linked to your identity in any way

If you choose to create an account to save your stats, we collect your email address and a display name. This data is stored securely and used only to provide the account feature.

Multiplayer and Room Codes

When you create or join a multiplayer game room, we temporarily store the room code, player display names chosen for that session, and game scores for the duration of the game. This data is deleted when the game session ends and is not linked to any persistent user profile unless you are signed in.

Daily Challenge

The Daily Trivia Challenge on our website uses your browser's local storage to track your daily streak and scores. This data lives only on your device and is never sent to our servers. No personal information is collected to play the Daily Challenge.

Trivia Night Generator

When you use the Trivia Night Generator, your category selections and preferences are sent to our server to generate questions. We do not log or retain this data after the response is returned to you. Payment for the Trivia Night Generator is processed by Stripe. We do not store your payment information directly. Stripe's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

Analytics

We may use anonymous, aggregated analytics to understand how the app is being used, such as which topics are most popular or how many games are played per day. This data does not identify individual users and is used only to improve Trivd.

Advertising

The free version of Trivd includes advertisements served through third-party ad networks. These networks may collect certain device identifiers and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies to serve relevant ads. You can remove all ads by upgrading to Trivd Pro.

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Age Requirement

Trivd is intended for users 13 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Third-Party Services

Our app and website use the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting and performance
  • Stripe: payment processing for the Trivia Night Generator
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: app distribution and any in-app purchases

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account credentials safe.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below and we'll respond within 7 business days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Trivd grows. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of the app or website after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
