Game Modes

Trivd has four game modes: Speed Duel (1v1 — first correct answer wins), Accuracy Mode (multiplayer — most correct answers wins), Bar Mode (2–6 players on one device, perfect for trivia nights and parties), and Chaos Mode (power-ups, point steals, and total mayhem). Each mode plays completely differently.

Speed Duel is a head-to-head 1v1 mode where both players see the same question at the same time. The first player to tap the correct answer wins the round. Speed matters just as much as knowledge — a buzzer-beater feels just as good as knowing the answer cold.

Bar Mode is designed for groups playing together in the same room on one device. 2–6 players take turns, questions are read aloud, and last place every round faces consequences decided by the group. No separate app download needed for other players — just pass the phone.

For Speed Duel and Accuracy Mode, your friends need the Trivd app downloaded to join. Create a room, share the room code, and they can join in seconds. For Bar Mode, only one person needs the app — everyone else just participates verbally.

Chaos Mode throws the standard rules out the window. It includes power-ups (like point multipliers and answer reveals), the ability to steal points from other players, and randomized modifiers that keep every round unpredictable. It's the mode for when you want a good time more than a fair fight.
