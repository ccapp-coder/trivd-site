    Questions

    Is the trivia night generator really free?

    Yes. Every sample round on this page is free, with no account and no sign-up. Tap Generate as many times as you like for a fresh set of 10 questions.

    How are the questions picked?

    The page draws 10 questions at random from a built-in bank, spread across the categories you select. Nothing is sent to a server, so it works instantly.

    What do I get with the $2.49 full trivia night?

    The full night builds 3, 5 or 7 rounds of 5 to 10 questions each on the topics you choose, or on witty topics picked for you, with a host answer sheet. It costs $2.49 per trivia night, paid securely through Stripe.

    Can I use these questions at a bar, school or party?

    Yes. Read them aloud, print the page, or put it on a screen. Tap Show Answers when it is time to score.

    How do I host a trivia night?

    Split players into teams, read each question twice, give about 30 seconds to write an answer, then reveal the answers at the end of the round. One point per correct answer, and the team with the most points wins.
